## Worth a post

**HAIARA Power Banks Recalled Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Fire and Burn Hazards; Sold on Amazon.com by Mitrytech** (CPSC 27056, 2026-10-29)
11,000 units · The power bank’s lithium battery can explode and ignite, posing fire and burn hazards to consumers.
Standards to look at: UL 2054 / IEC 62133-2 (portable packs), UL 2056 (power banks), UN 38.3

**Bose Recalls Lithium-Ion Battery Packs for S1 Pro and S1 Pro+ Bluetooth Speaker Systems Due to Risk of Injury from Fire and Overheating Hazards** (CPSC 27065, 2026-11-05)
634,759 units · The recalled lithium-ion battery packs can overheat and ignite, posing a risk of injury from fire and overheating hazards.
Standards to look at: UL 2054 / IEC 62133-2 (portable packs), UL 2056 (power banks), UN 38.3
https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Bose-Recalls-Lithium-Ion-Battery-Packs-for-S1-Pro-and-S1-Pro-Bluetooth-Speaker-Systems-Due-to-Risk-of-Injury-from-Fire-and-Overheating-Hazards-0

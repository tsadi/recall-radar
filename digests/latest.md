# Recall radar: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-08

3 relevant recalls · CPSC 2 · SafetyGate 1

## Worth a post

**Kobayashi Recalls Rechargeable Hand Warmers Due to Risk of Serious Injury from Fire and Burn Hazards** (CPSC 27011, 2026-10-01)
77,000 units · The lithium-ion battery and electrical components in the recalled hand warmers can overheat and ignite, posing a risk of serious injury from fire and burn hazards.
Standards to look at: UL 2054 / IEC 62133-2 (portable packs), UL 2056 (power banks), UN 38.3; UL 1310 / IEC 62368-1 (power supplies), UL 2202 / UL 2594 (EV)
https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Kobayashi-Recalls-Rechargeable-Hand-Warmers-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards

**Electrical Appliances: Wireless mouse** (SafetyGate SR/02757/26, 2026-10-07)
Environment. The solders in the product have excessive concentrations of lead and cadmium (measured values up to 61.2% and 0.297% by weight, respectively). Cadmium bio-accumulates and poses a risk to the environment.…
Standards to look at: UL 1310 / IEC 62368-1 (power supplies), UL 2202 / UL 2594 (EV)
https://ec.europa.eu/safety-gate-alerts/screen/webReport/alertDetail/10119110

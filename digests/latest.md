**Giant Bicycle Recalls LED-Light Keys Included with SureLock Bike Lock Sets Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Battery Ingestion; Violates Mandatory Standard for Consumer Products with Button Cell Batteries** (CPSC 27015, 2026-10-08)
3,630 units · The LED-light key that was included with certain SureLock Bicycle Lock sets violates the mandatory safety standard for consumer products with button cell and coin batteries because the button batteries can be accessed…
Standards to look at: UL 2054 / IEC 62133-2 (portable packs), UL 2056 (power banks), UN 38.3
https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Giant-Bicycle-Recalls-LED-Light-Keys-Included-with-SureLock-Bike-Lock-Sets-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Battery-Ingestion-Violates-Mandatory-Standard-for-Consumer-Products-with-Button-Cell-Batteries

**E-One Incorporated: Wire Harness and Busbar May Overheat** (NHTSA 26V652000, 2026-10-06)
301 units · An overheated busbar can increase the risk of a fire.
Standards to look at: UL 1310 / IEC 62368-1 (chargers and power supplies)
https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls?nhtsaId=26V652000

**Blue Bird Body Company: High Voltage Battery Mounting Bolts May Be Loose or Missing** (NHTSA 26V641000, 2026-10-02)
1,590 units · A high voltage battery with loose or missing mounting bolts can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Standards to look at: FMVSS 305a, UL 2580, ISO 6469-1, UN ECE R100
https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls?nhtsaId=26V641000

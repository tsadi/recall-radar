294 units · The recalled remote-controlled cars violate the mandatory standard for toys because they contain button cell and lithium-ion batteries in the remote control that can be easily accessed by children, posing a deadly…
Standards to look at: UL 2054 / IEC 62133-2 (portable packs), UL 2056 (power banks), UN 38.3

**Huffy Recalls Buzz Bicycles Cerana T2 E-Trikes Due to Risk of Serious Injury from Fall Hazard** (CPSC 27086, 2026-11-12)
286 units · The recalled tricycles’ rear wheels can become loose or separate from the rear frame, posing a risk of serious injury from a fall hazard.
Standards to look at: UL 2054 / IEC 62133-2 (portable packs), UL 2056 (power banks), UN 38.3
https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2027/Huffy-Recalls-Buzz-Bicycles-Cerana-T2-E-Trikes-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Fall-Hazard

**Electrical Appliances: Electric brush** (SafetyGate SR/03246/26, 2026-11-18)
Burns. The product becomes excessively hot when connected to the power supply and may therefore cause burns.
Standards to look at: UL 1310 / IEC 62368-1 (chargers and power supplies)
